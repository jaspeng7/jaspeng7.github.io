About this policy

MyQuarter is a personal goal and planning app for iPhone. It is made by Jason Peng, an independent developer based in Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, how long it is kept, and how you can see, change or delete it.

MyQuarter is in a free, invite-only beta. As the app changes, this policy will change with it.

The short version

  • What you write in MyQuarter is yours. We use it to run the app for you, and for nothing else.
  • We do not sell your information, show you ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Some features send parts of what you have written to an AI provider (currently Google's Gemini) to write a suggestion or a summary. Three of them run on their own. Switching off "AI suggestions" in Settings does not stop those three, or Ask my coach. All of them are listed below.
  • Your account and what you write are stored in Sydney, Australia. Some of the services we use are in other countries, mostly the United States.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you have written, at any time in Settings.

What we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account details: your email address and first name, and a password if you sign in with email. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider only in scrambled (hashed) form. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one, if you choose that.
  • What you write in the app: your vision across five life areas (physical, mental, relational, financial and professional), including your answers to prompts such as what you will no longer tolerate; your goals, due dates and lead actions; the days you tick an action off, and how many times; your habits and habit check-ins; your daily and weekly reflections; your priority cards, including any name you enter when you hand a card to someone; and how each goal turned out at the end of a season.
  • Your settings: theme, reminder and notification choices and times, time zone, and whether AI suggestions are on.
  • Information the app makes from yours: execution scores calculated from your ticks, and AI-written area summaries and weekly strengths and concerns, which are saved with your writing so they do not have to be written twice.

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage analytics: which screens you open and when you complete key steps, such as finishing onboarding, creating a goal, ticking off an action or asking for an AI suggestion. See "Analytics".
  • Technical information: your IP address, device model, iOS version, app version, and the times you use the app. Our database and analytics providers receive this as part of running their services.

Before you create an account: you can start writing your vision before you sign up. What you write is kept on your phone, and the app signs you in anonymously so the AI features can work. When you create your account, that anonymous sign-in becomes your account. If you never create one, the anonymous sign-in stays on our servers until we remove it. There is no automatic clean-up for these yet.

Sensitive information

The app asks you to think about your health, your money and your relationships. What you write may include health information or other information that privacy laws treat as sensitive.

We only have it because you choose to write it, and we only use it to provide the features you use. By writing it in the app, you agree to us storing it and, for the AI features described below, sending it to our AI provider to produce the result. Nothing sensitive is needed for the app to work, and you can edit or delete what you have written at any time.

We never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never share it with anyone other than the service providers listed below, who handle it on our behalf.

If you write about other people, such as a partner, a family member or a colleague, please keep it to what you need for your own planning.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: store your writing, calculate your scores, show your plans and reflections back to you, and keep your account secure.
  • To produce the AI features described below.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, using analytics.
  • To contact you about your account, the beta, or changes to this policy or our terms.
  • To meet our legal obligations and enforce our Terms of service.

The app makes no automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. The AI features only write suggestions and summaries for you to read.

AI features

Some features use an AI model to write a suggestion or a summary. The request goes from our server to the AI provider, and only the text that feature needs is sent. We do not add your name or email address to these requests, but your own writing is sent as you wrote it, including any names or details in it.

Our AI provider is currently Google (the Gemini API). Google may process these requests outside Australia, including in the United States, under its Gemini API terms. We use Gemini's paid service, so Google does not use what we send it, or what it sends back, to improve its products; it keeps them for a limited time to detect abuse. Anthropic (the Claude API) is set up as a backup and is only used if we switch to it. We do not train any AI model on your information.

What each feature sends:

  • Area summaries (automatic): when you move away from one area of your vision, that area's text is sent to write the one-line summary shown on its card. This happens without you tapping anything.
  • Weekly strengths and concerns (automatic): when you open a week's review, we send the week number, your execution score, the lead actions you committed to with the goal each belongs to and how many you completed, and the daily reflections you wrote that week. The result is saved on that week so it is only written once.
  • End-of-season goal summaries (automatic): when you reach the "Season complete" screen at the end of your twelve weeks, each goal's title, its score, and its lead actions with your week-by-week counts are sent to write a short summary.
  • Ask my coach (when you tap it): your answers to the vision prompts for every area you have written, plus the text you are writing at the time, are sent to write a short coaching note.
  • Suggest goals from your vision (when you tap it): the text of the vision area you choose, and the titles of the goals you already have, are sent to write goal ideas.
  • Suggest actions (when you tap it, while choosing a goal's weekly lead actions): the goal's title and due date, the names of the lead actions it already has, and the text of the vision area it belongs to (or just the goal's title, if it has none) are sent to write three suggested actions. Nothing is added to your goal unless you tap a suggestion.

The "AI suggestions" switch in Settings only hides "Suggest goals from your vision" and "Suggest actions". It does not stop the three automatic features or Ask my coach. If you would rather none of your writing were sent to an AI provider, email jaspeng6@gmail.com before you use the app and we will tell you what we can do.

AI providers may keep requests and responses for a limited time, for example to detect abuse, under their own terms.

Analytics

We use PostHog, on its United States cloud, to understand how the app is used, for example how many people finish onboarding or tick off an action. The app sends PostHog the name of each screen you open and named events such as "goal created" or "lead action ticked", sometimes with a number or a label: the week number, a score, or which life area or AI feature was used.

When you are signed in, these are linked to a random account ID. We never send PostHog your email address, your name, or anything you write.

Automatic tap capture, session replay and error capture are switched off, so PostHog does not record what is on your screen or what you type.

PostHog also receives your IP address, as any internet service does, and basic details about your device, such as its model, iOS version, language and app version. It may use the IP address to estimate a rough location.

PostHog keeps a random identifier on your phone so it can tell visits apart. It is replaced when you sign out or delete your account. We do not use analytics for advertising.

Emails and notifications

Account emails, such as password resets, are sent by our sign-in provider, Supabase.

Reminder emails: the app does not send reminder emails. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Notifications: if you allow them, the app's reminders (the weekly review, closing your day, a habit at risk) are scheduled by the app on your phone, from the notification choices in Settings, and shown by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them, and no push service is used. A habit reminder shows the habit's name, so it can appear on your lock screen. Signing out or deleting your account removes them from the phone.

If you test MyQuarter through TestFlight, Apple shares with us the crash reports and the feedback you choose to send through TestFlight, under Apple's own terms.

Storage on your phone

The app keeps some information on your phone so it works smoothly: your sign-in, kept in the iOS Keychain; your theme; which introductory screens you have seen; what you are writing before it saves (your name and vision before you sign up, today's reflection, a vision edit); and the last coaching notes and goal ideas the app showed you, together with the vision text they were written for. PostHog also keeps a random identifier, as described above.

Signing out removes your sign-in, and any unsaved reflection or vision edit, from the phone. Deleting your account removes everything the app keeps on the phone you delete it from.

Add to calendar: if you add a lead action to your calendar, the app opens Apple's own event screen, filled in with the action and its goal title. If you save it, the event goes into the calendar you choose, and that calendar's own service (for example iCloud or Google) receives it if it syncs. We save the date and time you picked so the app can show it back to you. The app does not read your calendar.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run parts of the app, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, account emails and server functions. Your account and everything you write are stored in Supabase's Sydney, Australia region. Server functions, including the ones that pass requests to our AI provider, may run outside Australia.
  • Google (Gemini API): the AI features.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): backup AI provider, only used if we switch to it.
  • PostHog: product analytics, on its United States cloud.
  • Google or Apple: sign-in, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Apple: TestFlight crash reports and feedback, if you test the beta.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or if it is needed to protect someone's safety. If MyQuarter is ever transferred to someone else, we will tell you before your information moves, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

Storing information overseas

Your account and your writing are stored in Australia. Some of the providers above handle information in other countries, mostly the United States. We use established providers whose terms include data protection commitments.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you write: until you delete it, or delete your account.
  • Anonymous sign-ins that never become an account: until we remove them. See "What we collect".
  • Copies held by our providers, such as server logs, short-term backups, analytics and AI provider records: for the limited time their own terms and settings allow. When an AI feature fails, server logs can occasionally contain part of the AI's response.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings. You type DELETE to confirm, and the deletion happens straight away. There is no grace period and it cannot be undone.

It permanently deletes your profile and everything you have written or recorded in the app: your vision, goals, lead actions, ticks, habits, weekly plans, daily and weekly reflections, priority cards, calendar entries, settings and notification choices.

It also deletes your sign-in record (your email address, name, password and any linked Google or Apple sign-in), removes your email address from the beta invite list, and clears what the app keeps on that phone. Copies held by our providers, as described above, are not removed by the button. To have your analytics deleted as well, email jaspeng6@gmail.com.

Your rights

You can see and edit most of your information in the app, and delete all of it in Settings. There is no self-service export yet. To get a copy of your data, to correct something, to ask what we hold about you, or to object to how we use it, email jaspeng6@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days and will not charge you. We may need to confirm the request comes from you first.

If you are not happy with our reply, you can complain to the privacy or data protection regulator where you live.

Keeping it secure

Database rules mean each account can only read and write its own records. Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). The keys for our AI provider are kept on the server and are never part of the app on your phone. The AI features only answer a signed-in session (including the anonymous one used before you create an account), and most are limited to a set number of uses per season.

As the operator, we can technically access the database. We only do so to maintain the app, fix a problem, respond to a request from you, or where the law requires it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you, and the regulator where the law requires it.

Children

You must be 18 or older to use MyQuarter. It is not directed at children or teenagers, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email jaspeng6@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as the app changes. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
